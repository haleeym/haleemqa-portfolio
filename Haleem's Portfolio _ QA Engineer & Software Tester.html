About Me

Behind every great product is quality that users can trust.

QA Detective

I am Haleem Ayira, a QA Engineer who specializes in manual functional testing, cross-browser and localization checks, and defect investigation.

My work is proof you can open: structured black-box testing on live web and mobile products, an internship at Sysbit Tech, and a group project where I led five testers. Every project here comes with the edge cases I found, the steps to reproduce them, and the documentation behind them.

My approach to testing is rooted in precision: writing thorough test suites, delivering clear defect documentation that saves developer time, and ensuring business requirements align with user experience.

I view software QA as more than just pointing out flaws—it is about bridging the gap between product managers and engineering teams. By translating complex user edge cases into clear, structured bug tickets, I help development teams fix critical issues faster and ship reliable software with confidence.

I'm open to remote QA roles and freelance projects. Whether you're hiring a tester or need a product checked before launch, I'm ready to start. My goal is to grow from manual QA into automation engineering, so more users get reliable software from every release.
